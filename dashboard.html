Key Insights

  • ●Pandemic Peak: Revenue surged 163% to $10.15M in 2020, driven by remote work demand.
  • ●2022 Contraction: Revenue dropped 46% to $4.96M; Q4 hit historic lows ($178K in Oct).
  • ●Volume-Driven: The decline is driven by 39.8% fewer orders, not lower AOV ($230 stable).
  • ●Loyalty Strength: Members outspent non-members by $44.80/order in 2022, representing 53.7% of transactions.
  • ●Product Concentration: Top 3 products generate 78.5% of total revenue ($22.06M).

Recommendations

  • ▸Deploy win-back campaigns targeting lapsed 2020 pandemic cohort customers.
  • ▸Secure inventory for Gaming Monitor, AirPods, MacBook Air (78.5% of revenue).
  • ▸Accelerate loyalty enrollment to capture $44.80 AOV premium per order.
  • ▸Audit laptop shipping to reduce 11%+ refund rates on ThinkPad & MacBook Air.
  • ▸Expand LATAM presence (only 6% share despite proximity to NA market).
